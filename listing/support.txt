Hold the Lace support

Hold the Lace keeps one pretend shoe lace. Tap Hold left, Hold right, Drop left, Drop right, Tie the bow, or Untie the bow. Both hands must hold the lace before the bow ties. The huge line says what the lace is doing.

The lace stays on this phone. The app does not use an account. For help, write to peaker.map@gmail.com.
