Hold the Lace is an offline pretend shoe lace. It starts loose. Hold left and Hold right put hands on the lace. Tie the bow works only when both hands are on it. Drop left and Drop right stay blocked while the bow is tied. Untie the bow lets the hands go again. New lace starts over after you confirm. The lace remains after you leave the app. There is no account, no camera, no clock, and no network use.
