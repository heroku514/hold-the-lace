Hold the Lace keeps one pretend shoe lace on the phone. It starts loose, with both hands down. A hand can hold the lace or let it go. The bow ties only when both hands are on it. A tied bow keeps both hands there until someone unties it. The huge line says the lace is loose, one hand is holding, it is ready to tie, or the bow is tied.

The lace stays on this phone. Start a new lace when you want both hands down again.

Hold the Lace does not use an account, ads, a camera, a clock, or a network connection. It does not teach a real knot. The lace in the app is original.
